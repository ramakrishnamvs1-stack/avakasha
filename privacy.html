myavakasha privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

myavakasha helps you find government jobs, private jobs, exams, scholarships, courses and trainings that match your education and details. This page explains what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

Your profiles, saved items, reminders and settings are stored only in your phone's browser storage. We do not have accounts and we do not keep a copy of your profile on any server. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your profile is gone unless you saved a backup file yourself.

Scanning documents

When you scan a marksheet, certificate, licence or work letter, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our reading service (hosted on Cloudflare). The service passes it to an AI model provided by Anthropic, which reads the text and returns the details to your phone. We do not store your photos or the text read from them. The reading service keeps only anonymous usage counters, such as how many scans were made in a day, to prevent misuse.

Scanning only happens after you tap to agree, and you can use the app without scanning by typing your details yourself.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, Aadhaar number or bank details.

Jobs and exams listed

Listings come from official government pages and, where marked, other public sites. They are checked automatically and by a person, but always confirm on the official notification before applying. myavakasha is not a government service.

Children

The app is meant for students and job seekers. If a child uses it, a parent or guardian should supervise any document scanning.

Your choices

You can delete a profile inside the app at any time, or clear the app's data to remove everything from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
